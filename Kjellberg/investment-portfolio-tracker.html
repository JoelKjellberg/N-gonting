Key Risks & Considerations

  • Storage Costs: Physical copper, silver, and gold require secure storage, insurance, and handling costs that can erode returns.

  • Currency Risk: INR/USD fluctuations can significantly impact returns for INR-denominated investors.
  • Liquidity: Physical commodities have lower liquidity compared to paper assets or crypto exchanges.
